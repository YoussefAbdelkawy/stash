Stash: four questions for the sections step

Each question shows option A and option B as a small phone. They're clickable: switch sections, type, tick steps, upload, change status. Example content only.

1 · How you add something

The + button opens a full page, as it does for videos today. Both options show the Save button turning into "Save as Draft" while required fields are missing, so you're never blocked.

A · Pick a section, then its formFive big tiles first; tap one to get that section's own form.
B · One form, section chips on topLands straight on the form. Tap a chip and the fields change in place.

2 · Certificates: when you earn one

Tap the certificate, then set its status to Done.

A · "You earned it" stepSetting Done opens a short celebration that asks for the proof file and credential link. You can skip and add them later.
B · Proof always in the sheetThe detail sheet always has a Proof section. Nothing pops up; fill it in whenever you like.

3 · Projects: editing the goal checklist

Tick steps and add new ones. Size and difficulty sit above the checklist.

A · Everything inlineTick a box to finish a step, × to remove, type at the bottom to add. No reordering.
B · Tick mode + Edit modeThe list is for ticking. "Edit steps" switches to rename, reorder (↑ ↓) and remove.

4 · Moving a shared link to the right section

A link shared from Chrome lands in Courses as a Draft. How do you say "this is actually a book"?

A · Section chips in every detail sheetEvery item's sheet starts with the section chips, so any item can be moved at any time.
B · A banner on drafts onlyOnly drafts show "Saved as a Course. Is it something else?" Once fixed, the banner goes away.
